Reviewer's guide

How it works

For a reviewer with fifteen minutes: where everything lives, how to rebuild it, where each number comes from, how it is proved, and the methods most worth challenging.

India Air Quality · Python, DuckDB, SQL, Excel, Power BI · 107 stations, 2019-01-01 to 2026-09-30 · read with the case study

1. The flow in one picture

The fifteen-minute review. The raw files and the warehouse are not in git (they are rebuilt from the archive), so a short review reads; a full proof rebuilds.

  1. Read sql/checks.sql next to src/mutation_test.py: one named check per rule, one deliberate break per check.
  2. Read the CPCB sub-index in sql/03_marts.sql against the breakpoint table in section 7, and the second, independent version inside checks.sql.
  3. Read the spike rule in src/config.py and its three marts at the end of 03_marts.sql.
  4. Open data/pipeline.log, mutation.log and the two .sha files after a build, or rebuild (section 3).

5. How it is proved

Checks gate the export

Each check in sql/checks.sql is a named query that returns the rows breaking its rule. pipeline.py runs all of them after the four SQL stages; if any returns a row it prints it, exports nothing and exits with an error. The checks recompute rather than restate; the PM2.5 and PM10 sub-indices are recomputed by a second, hand-written implementation of the CPCB bands. Mutation test

mutation_test.py holds one break per check and asserts the two lists match, so a check without a break fails the test. For each, it copies the warehouse to data/mutation.duckdb, confirms the check passes, applies the break (a deleted reading, an invented hour, a nudged sub-index, a station moved to another city...) and requires the check to return rows. The real warehouse is never touched; the run took 2.5 min.

Determinism

  • Exact numbers. Readings are stored as DECIMAL(18, 6), hourly and daily values as rounded decimals and the breakpoints as DECIMAL(10, 3). Sums are exact, so means do not depend on the order DuckDB's threads add them; no thread pinning is needed.
  • Fixed order. Every table is exported with ORDER BY ALL; the workbook uses fixed document dates and sorted rows; report object ids are hashes of their names, not random.
  • Proof. data/build_a.sha and build_b.sha hold the SHA-256 of the 18 outputs (17 CSVs and the workbook) after two builds from the raw files: 18 of 18 identical. render_docs.py re-hashes the current files against build B on every run.

To repeat it (Git Bash), hash the outputs after each of two builds from the raw files and compare:

sha256sum -b data/model/*.csv excel/india-air-quality.xlsx > data/build_b.sha
diff data/build_a.sha data/build_b.sha    # no output: identical

7. The AQI method (CPCB 2014)

CPCB, National Air Quality Index, CUPS/82/2014-15. Linear interpolation inside the band that holds the concentration, the published integer bands treated as continuous; at or above the top of Severe, 500:

I = I_lo + (I_hi - I_lo) / (B_hi - B_lo) * (C - B_lo), rounded to a whole number

  • Averaging. 24-hour mean for PM2.5, PM10, NO2 and SO2; for O3 and CO the highest 8-hour rolling mean whose window holds 6+ valid hours. A day needs 16 valid hours, or it gets no daily value and no sub-index.
  • Station AQI is the highest sub-index, published only with 3+ pollutants on complete days, one of them PM2.5 or PM10; that pollutant is the prominent one. City AQI is the rounded mean of that day's station AQIs, as in CPCB's bulletin.
  • Severe is open-ended in CPCB's table; here it closes at its lower bound plus the width of the Very Poor band (the last column). NH3 and lead are not in the archive.
  • Before any averaging, readings above a plausibility ceiling are set aside as faults: PM2.5 1,000, PM10 2,000, NO2 1,000, O3 1,000, SO2 2,000 µg/m³; CO 50 mg/m³. Exceedance uses the NAAQS: PM2.5 60, PM10 100, NO2 80, SO2 80 µg/m³ (24-hour); O3 100 µg/m³ (8-hour); CO 2 mg/m³ (8-hour).

8. The spike rule

Found from the data alone: no event calendar, no labels, no causes. The settings in src/config.py are written into the warehouse as ref_spike_rule, so the marts, the checks and the report read the same values.

SettingValueMeaning
SPIKE_MIN_RATIO2.0a spike day's city PM2.5 is at least this multiple of its reference
SPIKE_GUARD_DAYS3days either side left out of the reference, so an episode never sets its own
SPIKE_WINDOW_DAYS14the reference is the median of days 4 to 14 before and after
SPIKE_MIN_DAYS_EACH_SIDE6measured days needed on each side (of 11) for a day to be evaluated
SPIKE_MAX_DAYS3longest run of spike days that counts as a short spike
  1. mart_spike_days and mart_spike_runs: 144 of 5,724 evaluated city-days are spike days (2.5%), in 100 runs: 79 short spikes, 3 longer than 3 days and 18 next to a day without a reading or reference (length unknown). Only short spikes count; the rest keep their reason.
  2. mart_spike_episodes: each short spike's peak, reference and ratio, and the stations at 2.0x the city reference on the peak day. 22 are carried by one station of several; 2 rest on one reporting station.

9. Choices worth challenging

  • Set aside, never fill. Nothing is interpolated or estimated: 27 months stay empty, and every value shown was measured.
  • "ppb" relabelled, not converted. As true ppb, NO2 would jump at the gap and CO would fall to near zero. The evidence is a check: CO "ppb" median under 20; NO2 within 0.67 to 1.5 times its earlier level.
  • Conflicting duplicates: all copies set aside. Where two ids of one station disagree at one timestamp there is no basis to choose, so 475,812 readings go; averaging them would invent a value neither instrument reported.
  • A round spike threshold. Double the surrounding level, not a fitted value; change it in config.py and everything follows.

10. Known limits

  • No Indian data in the archive from 31 Oct 2022 to 19 Feb 2025; no weather or emissions data, so no causes.
  • Station sets grow and city AQI is a mean of stations, so city figures across years are not like-for-like.
  • Raw files and warehouse are not in git; no script writes the .sha files; discovery and the report checks are untimed.
  • Power BI has no free public link (the report ships as a project with images); the workbook has Excel Tables, not PivotTables.
